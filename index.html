How long does a typical website design & development project take?

Depends on the scope. A straightforward website usually takes 2 to 4 weeks. Something more complex with custom functionality or integrations can take 6 to 8 weeks. I'll give you a realistic timeline before we start, not an optimistic one.

Can you work from my existing Figma file?

Yes. Share the file and I'll build it out to match the design.

What does an n8n automation cost?

Depends on what you need. Simple automations start around $150 to $300. More complex ones with multiple steps, APIs, or conditional logic are scoped separately. I'll send you a clear number before anything starts.

Do I need to know anything technical to work with you?

No. You explain the problem in plain language, I handle the rest. I'll keep you updated the same way.

Will my site be easy to update after you're done?

Yes. Text, images, pages, you can handle all of it without touching code. Most clients are comfortable with it within the first day.

Do you offer ongoing support after launch?

Yes. The relationship does not have to end at launch. If you need updates, fixes, or new features, Rahul stays involved, and we can set up a monthly retainer once the project is done.

How do we get started?

Send me a message with a rough idea of what you need. I'll ask a few questions and if it looks like a good fit, we'll jump on a short call to sort out the details.
